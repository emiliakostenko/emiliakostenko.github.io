Self Assessment

Completing my coursework throughout the computer science program at SNHU and developing my portfolio has helped me identify and showcase my technical skills, refine my professional goals, and prepare to enter the computer science field. Building my ePortfolio and completing my capstone project has allowed me to apply concepts from across my degree to one project. This project showcases my skills in software design and development, data structures and algorithms, and databases. My coursework has helped me gain valuable hands-on experience in applying what I’ve learned. Through this program, I have gained skills in coding languages such as Python, Java, C++, SQL, and HTML. Additionally, my coursework has introduced me to various IDEs such as Jupyter Notebook, Visual Studio, Android Studio, and Codio. Furthermore, the coursework throughout this degree has helped me develop soft skills such as collaboration in a team environment and communicating with stakeholders. A few specific examples of courses that helped develop my technical and professional skills include:

  • CS305 helped me learn advanced security concepts and how to apply secure coding principles to development. This course also taught me how to apply encryption technologies and techniques to communicate securely.
  • DAT325 helped me gain an understanding of the importance of using high quality data in analysis, as well as gain experience communicating with team members to ensure requirements are clearly defined and met.
  • In CS250, I gained experience in applying SDLC stages with an Agile development environment and learned about the different roles involved in the development process. This class helped simulate what it would be like to work in a collaborative environment and communicate with different members of a team.
  • DAD220 helped me gain skills in creating structured database environments that incorporate basic processing functionality. CS340 helped me bridge the gap between server and the client by teaching me how to develop client/server applications.
  • CS465 taught me how to implement the MEAN stack to develop a full-stack application. This course helped me gain skills in being able to handle projects end-to-end, from the user interface to the database, and strengthened my overall problem-solving skills.
This portfolio contains an inventory tracking mobile app that I developed in CS360. The original version allowed users to perform CRUD operations for inventory items. Users were able to manage and keep track of inventory with screens for logging in, adding items, displaying current items, and managing item amounts. This app was created in October 2025. This portfolio showcases how I applied three rounds of enhancements to this artifact to demonstrate my skills in software design and engineering, data structures and algorithms, and databases. Together, these enhancements display my ability to iteratively improve a system, apply foundational computer science principles, and deliver a functional application.
